# Deckard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This is the Privacy Policy of Deckard, a companion app for Diablo IV made by Synica ("Synica", "we", "us" or "our"),
Verbeetenstraat 69B, 4812 XJ Breda, the Netherlands, registered with the Chamber of Commerce under number 81558643.
Synica is responsible for the personal data described here and processes it in accordance with the General Data
Protection Regulation (GDPR). Synica's website has its own privacy policy at synica.nl/privacy-policy.

Deckard is not made, endorsed or supported by Blizzard Entertainment.

## What stays on your computer

- **The game screen.** Deckard reads item tooltips and the zone name from the Diablo IV window, on your computer, using
  Windows' built-in text recognition. Screenshots are never sent anywhere.
- **Your builds, quick searches and settings.** Kept in a folder for your account on your computer, with the item values
  you correct when Deckard misreads one. When you import a
  build, Deckard downloads it from the site you linked: maxroll.gg, d4builds.gg (whose builds are stored with Google
  Firebase) or Icy Veins.
- **Your session.** Kept in Windows' Credential Locker, so you stay signed in.

## What Deckard's trade server keeps, and why

**Your account.** Your Battle.net account ID and BattleTag, from signing in with Battle.net. We never see your e-mail
address or password. We use them to know who you are; if you change your BattleTag, your listings stay yours.
*Basis: providing the service you signed up for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).*

**Your sign-ins.** When each of your sessions started and when it runs out, so that signing out ends the session on our
server too and a copy of it stops working. *Basis: providing the service.*

**Your trades.** Your listings (the item's type, name, item power, affixes, aspect, price and realm), offers,
counter-offers, accepted deals, buy orders and the price a listing sold for. We use them to run the market. Past sale
prices appear in price checks and price history without saying who sold or bought. *Basis: providing the service.*

**Your trading record.** Whether each side marked a deal completed, and the text of any report about a deal. Your
record is shown to the players you trade with, and reports are used for moderation. *Basis: our legitimate interest
in a safe market (art. 6(1)(f)).*

**Players you block.** So that you and they no longer see each other. *Basis: providing the service.*

**Whether you are in game.** Only if you turn on "Show me as in game" or location sharing in Settings: whether Diablo IV
is running and when you were last seen, shown to other traders as "in game". *Basis: providing the feature you turned
on; turn it off in Settings at any time.*

**Your in-game zone.** Only if you turn on location sharing: the name of the zone Diablo IV shows (for example
"Kyovashad"), shown only to the player whose offer you accepted, and only while that deal is open, so you can meet in
game. We keep only your latest zone, for 10 minutes. This is your location in the game, never your real-world
location. *Basis: providing the feature you turned on.*

**Your Patreon link.** Only if you link Patreon: your Patreon user ID, whether you are an active patron of Deckard's
campaign, whether your tier includes early access to new versions of the app, and when we last checked. It lifts app
limits for supporters and never gives an advantage in the market. We never see your name, e-mail address or payment
details. *Basis: providing the service.*

**Your name in the credits.** Only if you are a patron and choose to be named: the name you enter is shown in Deckard's
credits to every signed-in player while you are a patron. Turn it off in Settings at any time; unlinking Patreon removes
it. *Basis: your consent (art. 6(1)(a)).*

**Usage statistics.** Only if you agree when Deckard asks, and you can turn it off in Settings at any time: a random id
for your copy of Deckard (not linked to your account), the app and Windows versions, and events such as starting the
app, a crash with its error details, how many lines of an item tooltip were read and at what screen size, and which
page you opened. Never your BattleTag, what you trade, your screen or your IP address. We use it to find bugs and see
which features are used. *Basis: your consent (art. 6(1)(a)).*

**Bug reports and feature requests.** Only when you send one from Deckard: its title and description, the diagnostics
you choose to include (app and Windows versions, screen size, recent errors), and up to 3 screenshots. Deckard blacks
out BattleTags, chat and party names on your computer before anything is sent, and shows you the result first; check it,
because names above characters in the game can be missed. We file your report as a public issue in Deckard's
open-source repository on GitHub: anyone can read its title, description and the diagnostics you include, so don't
write personal details in them. The issue carries no BattleTag or account, only a report number. Screenshots stay on our server, stripped
of any hidden data, and never go to GitHub. We keep which account sent which report, to stop abuse. *Basis: our
legitimate interest in fixing and improving Deckard (art. 6(1)(f)).*

**IP addresses.** Used briefly, in memory, to limit how many requests one player can make, and kept in our hosting
provider's logs. *Basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the service available.*

Your BattleTag is shown to another player only when one of you accepts the other's offer, so you can add each other in
game. Search results and buy orders never show it.

Deckard never handles real money: trades are priced in gold and completed by hand in the game.

## Who else processes your data

- **Blizzard Entertainment**, for Battle.net sign-in, under Blizzard's own privacy policy.
- **Patreon**, if you link it, under Patreon's own privacy policy.
- **Microsoft Azure**, which hosts the trade server and its database in the Netherlands (West Europe region).
- **maxroll.gg, d4builds.gg (and Google Firebase, which stores its builds) and Icy Veins**, when Deckard downloads a
  build you asked for. They see your IP address and the build, as when you open it in a browser.
- **GitHub** (Microsoft), where reported bugs and feature requests are tracked as public issues, without your name or account.

We do not rent or sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## International transfer

The trade server and its data are in the Netherlands. If you use Deckard from elsewhere, your
data is transferred there.

## Legal requests and preventing harm

We may keep and share data when the law requires it, for example because of a court order, or when it is needed to
detect and stop fraud or other abuse of the market, such as reported scams. Data involved in such a request or
investigation may be kept longer than the periods below.

## Security

The trade server is reached only over encrypted connections, every request needs a signed-in player, and secrets such
as passwords are kept out of the code. No system is perfectly secure; if you find a problem, please tell us at
info@synica.nl.

## Change of control

If Synica or Deckard is sold or transferred, your data may be transferred with it. The new owner must keep the
commitments in this policy.

## How long we keep your data

- A sign-in or Patreon link in progress (the port Deckard listens on for the answer and, once Battle.net or Patreon has
  answered, your account ID): until it finishes, 10 minutes at most.
- Your sign-ins: until you sign out or delete your account, 7 days at most.
- Your in-game zone: 10 minutes.
- Usage statistics: 90 days; after that only daily totals per event and app version remain, with no id.
- Screenshots sent with a report: 90 days after its GitHub issue is closed (a year at most if it was never filed).
- Which account sent a report: 12 months, once its screenshots are deleted. The public GitHub issue, which names no one, stays.
- Listings, offers, deals, buy orders and reports: 12 months after the listing, deal or buy order ends. Open deals are
  kept until they end.
- Your account and Patreon link: until you delete your account in Settings (or ask us to), or unlink Patreon.
- Your name in the credits: until you turn it off or unlink Patreon.

## Your rights

In Deckard's Settings you can download everything our trade server keeps about you, and delete your account: that
removes your listings, offers, buy orders, blocks, sign-ins, Patreon link, credit name and reports, with their screenshots, at
once. An account banned for abuse, or under review, keeps only its Battle.net ID, BattleTag and the ban, so deleting
it can't undo the ban. Usage statistics aren't linked to your account; turn them off in Settings. You can also ask us to
show, correct, export or delete your data, and to restrict or stop using it, by e-mailing info@synica.nl. Unlinking Patreon in Settings removes the Patreon link at once. If you are unhappy with how we handle
your data, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens,
autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

## Children

Deckard is not meant for anyone under 13, and you need a Battle.net account under Blizzard's terms to use it. We do not
knowingly keep data about children under 13. If you know a child has used Deckard, please contact us and we will
remove their data.

We may update this policy. The new version is published here with its date, and important changes are also announced
in the app.
