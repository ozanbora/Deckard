# Deckard: frequently asked questions

*Draft for synica.nl, to review before publishing.*

## What is Deckard?

A companion app for Diablo IV on Windows. It lays out a build from maxroll, d4builds or Icy Veins item by item, tracks your gear and what's
left to craft, walks you through the skill tree and Paragon boards while you level, reads items when you hold Alt over
them in game and tells you whether to keep, craft, sell or salvage them, and has a market where players trade items
for gold.

## Can I get banned for using Deckard?

Deckard is built to stay outside the game: it only reads what's on your screen, the way a screen recorder does. It never
reads the game's memory, never changes its files, never hooks into it and never presses keys or clicks for you. Its
overlay is its own window on top of the game.

That said, Blizzard hasn't reviewed or approved Deckard, and only Blizzard decides what its rules allow. We know of no
bans for screen-reading overlays, but we can't promise anything on Blizzard's behalf. Read Blizzard's terms and decide
for yourself.

## Does Deckard automate anything in the game?

No. You move items, trade and play yourself. Deckard never sends input to the game, and never will for Diablo IV.

## Is trading for real money?

No. The market is for gold only. Buying or selling items for real money breaks Blizzard's terms, and Deckard has no
way to do it. You meet your trade partner in game and trade there by hand.

## What do I need?

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
- Diablo IV in windowed or borderless mode (Windows can't capture exclusive fullscreen).
- A Battle.net account to sign in.
- An English game client. *(To confirm before release: other languages are untested.)*

## Why do I sign in with Battle.net?

So trades happen between real Battle.net accounts, and so your builds and settings follow you to another PC. Deckard
only gets your BattleTag and an account number from Battle.net, never your password.

## What does Deckard know about me?

See the [privacy policy](https://www.synica.nl/deckard-privacy-policy) and the [terms of use](https://www.synica.nl/deckard-terms-of-use). In short: your BattleTag, your listings, offers and trade records, your
settings, and, only if you turn them on, whether you're in game, your zone for an accepted trade partner, your Patreon
link, and anonymous usage data.

## Where do builds come from?

From maxroll.gg (build guides and planners), d4builds.gg (build pages) and Icy Veins (planner links: on an Icy Veins
guide, open its planner and copy that link). Paste the link into Deckard and import it. d4builds builds come without
skill points; everything else carries over. Deckard isn't affiliated with any of these sites.

## Is Deckard free?

Yes. Patrons on Patreon get conveniences (more saved searches and builds, stash sorting against every build, early
access for Champions, their name in the credits), never an advantage in the market.

## How do I uninstall Deckard?

From Windows' *Installed apps*. Your builds and settings stay in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Deckard` in case you come back; delete
that folder to remove them too. To delete what Deckard's server keeps about you, use *Delete my account* in Settings
before uninstalling.

## Something doesn't work. What do I do?

Use **Send feedback** in Deckard's title bar: describe it and add a screenshot. Deckard covers anything private in the
screenshot before sending, and you can check it first.
