## 1.0.0 (20 November 2026)

The first public release. Deckard is now open source under the GNU AGPL v3.0, the trade server included; About
shows the version, the license and a link to the source.

- **Builds:** load a build from a maxroll guide or planner, d4builds.gg or an Icy Veins planner link; the build's items
  laid out like the character screen, with the seal, charms, skill bar, the runes and gems it sockets, and how-to-play
  notes; a library of your builds.
- **Talisman and mercenaries:** the seal and charms with their set bonuses, and the guide's mercenaries with their skill
  picks marked on the game's own mercenary panel.
- **Gear tracking:** hover your worn items and see how close they are to the build and what's left to craft.
- **Skill tree and Paragon:** the build's points step by step while you level, and the build highlighted on the in-game
  skill tree and paragon boards: its nodes, which glyph goes in each socket, and the next board to attach, outlined
  where it goes and turned as the build has it.
- **Reading items:** press Alt (or your chosen key) over an item in game for its fit to your build: keep, craft or
  salvage; click a misread value to correct it; runes you hover are remembered, with runeword advice for empty sockets.
- **Stash sorting:** sort a stash session's items into keep, craft, sell and salvage.
- **Trading for gold:** search, listings, offers and counter-offers, buy orders, price checks and price history, trading
  records, blocking, and trade notices over the game.
- **Patreon:** link your Patreon account; patrons get more saved searches and builds, stash sorting against every build,
  early access (Champions) and their name in the credits.
- **Feedback:** send bug reports and feature requests from the app, with screenshots covered where they might show
  something private.
- **Privacy:** anonymous usage and crash data, only if you agree; download or delete everything the trade server keeps
  about you from Settings. Signing out now also ends the session on the server. Bug reports become public GitHub issues
  (screenshots stay private on the server).
