## Deckard

**A companion app for Diablo IV.** Reads your loot. Checks it against your build. Trades it for gold.

**[Download for Windows]** *(Setup.exe, about N MB, version X.Y.Z)*

Installs for your Windows user only, without administrator rights, and keeps itself up to date.

### What it does

- **Your build, item by item.** Import a build from maxroll, d4builds or Icy Veins and see every item it needs.
- **Your gear and what's left to craft.** Deckard tracks how close your worn gear is and what to imprint, enchant or temper.
- **Skill tree and Paragon, step by step** while you level.
- **Reads your loot.** Hold Alt over an item in game: keep it, craft it, sell it or salvage it?
- **Sorts your stash** against the builds you play.
- **Trades for gold** with other players: search by affix, make offers, post buy orders. You trade by hand in game.

### What it doesn't do

Deckard only reads what's on your screen. It never reads the game's memory, never touches its files and never presses a
key for you. Trading is for gold only, never real money.

### System requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- Diablo IV in windowed or borderless mode
- A Battle.net account
- An English game client *(to confirm before release)*
